Survive escalating waves of hostiles — some shoot back, some fly, some carry shields you'll need to flank. Walk into low crates or rocks to automatically climb onto them. Health regenerates after a few seconds without damage; medkits, ammo, and grenade pickups also spawn during a run. You carry 10 nukes — each clears every enemy on the map.
